# V12 theme-scene generation notes

Mode: OpenAI built-in image generation.

All six deployed assets were generated as original, polished 3D decorative vignettes
for a dark fitness-portal background. The shared direction required a near-black `#08090c`
backdrop, the upper roughly 45% left quiet, objects concentrated along the lower edge,
soft side fades, dramatic but restrained rim lighting, and no text, logos, people,
watermarks, or UI elements. Source PNGs were converted to quality-82 JPEGs for the
deployed PWA.

## Prompt set

- `halloween-scene.jpg`: carved glowing jack-o'-lantern beside a black iron cauldron
  with violet smoke, small candles, autumn leaves, and orange/violet rim light.
- `thanksgiving-scene.jpg`: realistic turkey, leather football, pumpkins, wheat, and
  an autumn tree with orange leaves, arranged as a warm amber/copper vignette.
- `christmas-scene.jpg`: snow-dusted decorated Christmas tree, seated Santa figure,
  wrapped gifts, lantern, and restrained red/green/gold highlights.
- `valentine-scene.jpg`: metallic heart sculpture, red/pink roses, teddy bear, heart
  balloons, petals, and soft pink/red rim light.
- `new-year-scene.jpg`: midnight clock, silver/gold ornaments, confetti, fireworks,
  and restrained icy-blue celebration light.
- `independence-scene.jpg`: folded American flag, metallic stars, sparklers, and
  restrained red, white, and blue fireworks.
