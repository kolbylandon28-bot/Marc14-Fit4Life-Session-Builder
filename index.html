Beginner Glossary

Plain-language definitions for the terms used throughout the workouts.

Quick guide
Working set

A set that counts toward the workout after the lighter warm-up sets are finished.

RPE

Effort from 1–10. RPE 8 means you could perform about two more clean reps.

RIR

“Reps in reserve”—the number of good repetitions you believe you had left.

Tempo

How quickly each part of a repetition is performed, especially the lowering phase.

Superset

Two exercises performed back-to-back before taking a full rest.

Drop set

Perform a hard set, reduce the weight, and immediately continue with more reps.

Finisher

A short final exercise intended to challenge the trained muscles or conditioning.

Deload

An easier training week that reduces fatigue while keeping the movement routine.
